Installation finished!

Open Autodesk Revit, click the "Node.aec" tab and then the "Hello World"
button in the "Sample Plugin" panel.

The command verifies your license locally (no network call) through the
NodeAec.Licensing.Lite package that ships with the plugin: with a valid
license it shows the greeting and the license details; without one it
explains why and offers to open the Node.aec Connector (to sign in,
activate or renew).

If the button does not show up, make sure you ran the installer matching
your Revit year (each installer serves exactly one year group).
