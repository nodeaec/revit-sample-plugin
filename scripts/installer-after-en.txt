Installation finished!

Open Autodesk Revit, click the "Node.aec" tab and then the "Hello World"
button in the "Sample Plugin" panel.

The command checks your license with the Node.aec Connector before running:
with a valid license it shows the greeting and the license details; without
one it explains why and offers to open the Connector.

If the button does not show up, make sure you ran the installer matching
your Revit year (each installer serves exactly one year).
